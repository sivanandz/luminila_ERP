Is this real gold jewellery?

No. Niththigal is an imitation jewellery concept, with gold-tone, heritage-inspired designs—not solid gold. The catalogue shown here is a preview; materials and specifications must be confirmed before purchase.

How do I care for my jewellery?

Keep imitation jewellery away from water, perfume and harsh chemicals. Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth after wearing and store each piece separately in a dry pouch. Always follow the final product's specific care instructions.

How do I choose a bangle size?

Measure the inside diameter of a well-fitting bangle. Common sizes are 2.4 (57.2 mm), 2.6 (60.3 mm) and 2.8 (63.5 mm). Choose your size in the product details. If you're between sizes, confirm the fit with the store before ordering.

Can I place an order on this website?

This is a storefront preview. You can explore the collection, save favourites and try the shopping bag. Prices, imagery and stories are illustrative. Live stock, shipping, returns and secure payments will need to be connected before orders can be placed.
