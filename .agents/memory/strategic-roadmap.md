# Strategic Feature Roadmap & Competitive Benchmark

## Core Focus: Imitation & Fashion Jewelry
- **Primary Domain:** High-velocity imitation / fashion jewelry retail & wholesale.
- **Active Roadmap Priorities:** **Option B** (WhatsApp Commerce 2.0) + **Option D** (Avant-Garde AI Visual Commerce).


## Competitive Gap Analysis (vs WinGold, Marg ERP, JewelERP)
1. **Live Metal Rate Engine**: Dynamic gold/silver/platinum calculation: `(Net Wt × Spot Rate) + Making Charges + Stones`.
2. **Old Gold / Trade-In POS Module**: Gross weight, melt loss, purity % deduction applied directly to customer invoice.
3. **Artisan / Karigar Job-Work Tracker**: Custom orders, repair intake, artisan metal loss reconciliation, before/after photos.
4. **Jewelry Savings Schemes (11+1 Advance Deposit)**: Customer monthly installments with store bonus redemption.
5. **HUID & Lab Certificate Vault**: BIS 6-digit hallmarking and diamond lab certificate attachment.

## Novel Market Differentiators (First-in-Market)
1. **WhatsApp Commerce 2.0**: Native interactive WhatsApp Flows, timed flash drops to `@newsletter` channels with live paylinks.
2. **AI "Match My Outfit" Stylist**: Customer sends dress photo on WhatsApp; Vision AI recommends matching jewelry sets with channel prices.
3. **Computer Vision Counter Audit**: Camera scan of velvet display trays for rapid morning/closing item reconciliation.
4. **Digital Luxury Passport / NFC**: Wallet pass with authenticity certificates and guaranteed buyback valuation.
